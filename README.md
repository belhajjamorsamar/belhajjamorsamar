## 🔭 Currently Building — retail-agentic-copilot

A multi-agent system (LangGraph) for retail: a **RAG-based customer service agent** and a **dynamic anti-waste pricing agent**, orchestrated by a supervisor agent. Built around real Open Food Facts product data plus clearly-documented synthetic sales/inventory data.

**Why this project:** supermarkets deal with stockout/overstock, food waste near expiration dates, and fragmented customer service — this tackles the last two with a real multi-agent architecture, not a toy demo.

**Status:** Phase 1 (data layer) complete ✅ — catalog ingestion, cleaning/validation with Pydantic, synthetic sales & inventory generation. Currently building Phase 2 (RAG customer service agent).

**Stack:** LangGraph · OpenAI GPT-4o-mini · ChromaDB · text-embedding-3-small · Pydantic

🔗 [Repo](https://github.com/belhajjamorsamar/retail-agentic-copilot)
